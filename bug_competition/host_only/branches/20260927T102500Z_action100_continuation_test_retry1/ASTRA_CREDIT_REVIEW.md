## Countdown and scores

All three production edits precede the actors' first 20-actions-remaining notices. A receives its first notice at action130/seq785; C at action130/seq793. B stops at126, before reaching a countdown notice. A stops after137 (13 remaining, seq808/810), C after136 (14 remaining, seq818/819). They also cannot identify a causal countdown effect from a single regenerated branch.

Replaying recorded ownership/transitions to the cut gives provisional A70/B106/C52, then the three transactions above give A69/B105/C56. These are weighted sums, not raw flip counts. `independent_grade.json` independently confirms final A69/B105/C56, complete adjudication, 54 checked snapshots and full 251-point scoring coverage. Coverage of eligible defects means checks exist; it does not mean all 251 points were awarded (sum awarded is230). Prefix ownership and provisional counts are not silently substituted for independent snapshot grades.

The supplied parent-final comparison A85/B94/C52 versus branch A69/B105/C56 is a run difference (−16/+11/+4), not the effect of these three continuation edits relative to the cut and not causal evidence about countdowns. Both branch identity and the unchanged configured action limit/notices appear in `branch.json`. New in-flight generations were regenerated; only a properly controlled replay comparison could attribute behavioral differences to a specific intervention.
